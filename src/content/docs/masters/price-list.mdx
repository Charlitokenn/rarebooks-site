A price list keeps a set of item rates. Use price lists to sell or buy the same items at different rates, such as wholesale and retail rates. When you select a price list on an invoice, RareBooks takes the item rates from the list.

## Turn On Price Lists

1. Click **Setup** > **Settings** in the sidebar.
2. In the **General** tab, turn on **Enable Price List**.
3. Click **Save**, and then click **Yes** to reload RareBooks.

**Price List** now shows in the **Common** group of the sidebar. Sales invoices, sales quotes, and purchase invoices show a **Price List** field. If you turn off price lists later, RareBooks takes the rates from the items again.

## Create a Price List

Only a **Books Manager** or a **System Manager** can make and change price lists.

![Price list Wholesale for sales, with item prices for six items.](/images/docs/books-docs-price-list-1-price-list-form-v4.png)

1. Click **Common** > **Price List** in the sidebar.
2. Click **New**.
3. Enter a **Name**, such as `Wholesale`. You cannot change it after you save.
4. Select **For Sales**, **For Purchase**, or both. **For Sales** is selected by default.
5. In **Item Prices**, click **Add row**. Select the **Item** and enter the **Rate**. On a phone, fill in the row in the sheet that opens, and tap **Done**.
6. Add a row for each item that has a price in this list.
7. Click **Save**.

| Field | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Is Price List Enabled | Only enabled price lists show on invoices. A new list is enabled. |
| For Sales | Use the list on sales invoices, sales quotes, and in the POS. |
| For Purchase | Use the list on purchase invoices. |
| Unit Type | In an item price row. The unit of the price. It starts as the unit of the item. |
| Rate | In an item price row. The price of one unit, in your company currency. |

Add only one price for each item and unit. If **Enable UOM Conversion** is on, you can add a price for each unit of an item, such as `Unit` and `Box`. See [Inventory Settings](/inventory/inventory-settings/).

## Use a Price List on an Invoice

1. Open a draft sales invoice, sales quote, or purchase invoice.
2. In **Price List**, select the price list.
3. Add the items.

The field shows only enabled lists for that side: **For Sales** lists on sales documents, and **For Purchase** lists on purchase invoices. A return keeps the price list of its original invoice.

When you select an item, RareBooks gets the rate in this order:

1. The price in the price list for the unit of the row.
2. The price in the price list for the unit of the item, converted to the unit of the row.
3. The **Rate** of the item.

For an invoice in a different currency, RareBooks converts the rate with the exchange rate. See [Multi-Currency Invoicing](/transactions/multicurrency-accounting/).

RareBooks gets the rates again when you change the **Price List**, the party, the date, the currency, or the exchange rate. It also gets the rate of a row again when you change its unit. If you enter a rate in a row yourself, RareBooks keeps it.

Pricing rules apply after the price list. A rule can replace the rate or give a discount on it. See [Pricing Rule](/masters/pricing-rule/).

## Use a Price List in the POS

1. Click **Price list** above the totals of the cart. On a phone, tap the **…** button in the header, and then tap **Price List**.
2. Select a **Price List**.
3. Click **Apply**.

RareBooks gets the rates of the items in the cart again. To stop using the price list, click **Remove price list**, and then click **Apply**. On a phone, tap the delete button next to the field. See [Point of Sale (POS)](/transactions/point-of-sale/).
