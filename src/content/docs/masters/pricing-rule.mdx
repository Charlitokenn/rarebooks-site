A pricing rule changes the price of an item on a sales invoice when the invoice meets the conditions of the rule. Use them for quantity discounts, free items, special rates, and offers between two dates. Pricing rules apply to sales invoices and to POS sales. They do not apply to sales quotes, purchase invoices, or returns.

## Turn On Pricing Rules

Pricing rules need discount accounting.

1. Click **Setup** > **Settings** in the sidebar.
2. In the **General** tab, turn on **Enable Discount Accounting**. **Enable Pricing Rule** then shows.
3. Turn on **Enable Pricing Rule**.
4. Click **Save**, and then click **Yes** to reload RareBooks.

**Pricing Rule** now shows in the **Sales** group of the sidebar.

:::caution
You cannot turn off discount accounting after you save it. See [Discount Accounting](/transactions/discount-accounting/).
:::

You can turn off pricing rules later. RareBooks then stops applying them. Submitted invoices keep their prices.

## Create a Pricing Rule

Only a **Books Manager** or a **System Manager** can make and change pricing rules.

![Pricing rule Buy 3 Notebooks, Get 1 Free with the discount type Product Discount, priority 1, and the applied item Spiral Notebook.](/images/docs/books-docs-pricing-rule-1-rule-form-v4.png)

1. Click **Sales** > **Pricing Rule** in the sidebar.
2. Click **New**.
3. Enter a **Title**, such as `Buy 5, get 10% off`.
4. In **Applied Items**, click **Add row** and select the **Item**. Add a row for each item of the offer.
5. Select the **Discount Type**, and then fill in the fields of the discount.
6. Select the **Priority**.
7. Optional: Set the quantity, amount, and date conditions.
8. Click **Save**.

On a phone, **Add row** opens a sheet. Fill in the row and tap **Done**.

## Pricing Rule Fields

| Field | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Is Pricing Rule Enabled | Only enabled rules apply. A new rule is enabled. |
| Discount Type | **Price Discount** changes the rate or gives a discount. **Product Discount** gives a free item. |
| Is Coupon Code Based | The rule applies only when the invoice has a coupon code for the rule. Shows only when coupon codes are on. See [Coupon Code](/masters/coupon-code/). |
| Priority | A number from 1 to 20. See [Priority](#priority). |

### Applied Items

Each row has an **Item** and a **Unit Type**. The **Unit Type** starts as the stock unit of the item. You can change it to a unit in the **UOM Conversions** of the item.

The rule applies only to invoice rows whose **Transfer Unit** is the **Unit Type**. A row without a **Transfer Unit** is in the stock unit. To apply the rule to two units, add two rows with the same item, such as one for `Unit` and one for `Box`.

### Price Discount

Select the **Price Discount Type**, and then enter its value.

| Price Discount Type | What the Rule Does to Each Row of the Item |
| --- | --- |
| Rate | Sets the rate to the **Rate**, for one unit of the **Unit Type**. If you entered a rate in the row, RareBooks keeps your rate. |
| Discount Percentage | Sets the discount percent of the row, from 0 to 100. |
| Discount Amount | Sets the discount amount of the full row, not of one unit. If it is more than the row amount, the discount is the row amount. |

A discount from a rule replaces a discount that you entered in the row. RareBooks posts it to the discount account.

### Product Discount

| Field | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Free Item | The item that the customer gets free. It can be the same item as in **Applied Items**. |
| Quantity | The quantity of the free item, in the **UOM**. |
| UOM | The unit of the free row. If it is empty, RareBooks uses the unit of the free item. |
| Round Free Item Quantity | Rounds the free quantity to a whole number with the **Rounding Method**: **Floor** (down), **Round** (nearest), or **Ceil** (up). |
| Is Recursive | Gives the free item again for each set of items that the customer buys. |
| Recurse Every (As Per Stock Unit) | The number of items in each set, in the stock unit. |

RareBooks adds one free row with a rate of zero, also when the item is on more than one row.

With **Is Recursive**, the free quantity is the **Quantity** multiplied by the item quantity, divided by **Recurse Every**. For example, with **Quantity** `1` and **Recurse Every** `3`, 7 notebooks give 2.33 free notebooks. With the rounding method **Floor**, they give 2. For units that allow only whole quantities, such as `Unit`, select **Round Free Item Quantity**. If the free quantity is zero after rounding, the rule does not apply.

### Conditions

| Field | The Rule Applies When |
| --- | --- |
| Min Qty, Max Qty (As Per Stock Unit) | The total quantity of the item is in this range. |
| Min Amount, Max Amount | The total amount of the item is in this range. |
| Valid From, Valid To | The date of the invoice is in this range. |

The totals count only the rows in a **Unit Type** of the rule. A zero or empty value means no limit on that side. **Min Amount** must be less than **Max Amount**.

Enter the **Rate**, the **Discount Amount**, and the amount limits in your company currency. For an invoice in a different currency, RareBooks converts them with the exchange rate.

## Priority

More than one rule can apply to the same item. RareBooks then uses the rule with the highest **Priority**. The highest priority is 20.

If two rules have the same highest priority for an item, RareBooks shows an error, and you cannot save the invoice. Give each rule for the same item a different priority.

## How RareBooks Applies Pricing Rules

RareBooks applies pricing rules while you edit a sales invoice, and again when you save and submit it. For each row, it applies the matching rule with the highest priority. Each row gets one pricing rule at most. If a condition stops matching, RareBooks removes the free item or the price change. The **Pricing Rule Detail** table of the invoice shows each rule that applied.

### Pricing Rules in the POS

In the POS cart, a free item shows the name of its pricing rule. You cannot edit a free item.

To stop pricing rules in the POS, turn on **Ignore Pricing Rule** in the POS settings or in the POS profile. A pricing rule can change the rate or the discount also when **Can Change Rate** or **Can Change Discount** is off. See [Point of Sale (POS)](/transactions/point-of-sale/).

## Example: Buy 3, Get 1 Free

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Applied Items | `Notebook` |
| Discount Type | Product Discount |
| Free Item | `Notebook` |
| Quantity | 1 |
| Round Free Item Quantity | Selected, with **Floor** |
| Is Recursive | Selected |
| Recurse Every (As Per Stock Unit) | 3 |
| Min Qty (As Per Stock Unit) | 3 |

![Product discount fields: free item Spiral Notebook, quantity 1, rounding Floor, Is Recursive, Recurse Every 3, and Min Qty 3.](/images/docs/books-docs-pricing-rule-2-free-item-settings-v4.png)

A row with 7 notebooks gets a row with 2 free notebooks.
