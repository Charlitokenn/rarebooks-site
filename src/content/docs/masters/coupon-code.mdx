A coupon code applies a pricing rule to a sales invoice. The customer gives you the code, and you add it to the invoice. The pricing rule of the coupon then gives the discount or the free item.

## Turn On Coupon Codes

Coupon codes need discount accounting and pricing rules.

1. Click **Setup** > **Settings** in the sidebar.
2. In the **General** tab, turn on **Enable Discount Accounting**, **Enable Pricing Rule**, and **Enable Coupon Code**.
3. Click **Save**, and then click **Yes** to reload RareBooks.

**Coupon Code** now shows in the **Sales** group of the sidebar, and sales invoices have a **Coupons** section. You cannot turn off discount accounting after you save it. If you turn off coupon codes later, RareBooks does not save sales invoices that have coupons.

## Set Up a Coupon Code

Each coupon applies one pricing rule, and the rule must be coupon based. Only a **Books Manager** or a **System Manager** can make coupon codes.

![Coupon code DIWALI10 with its pricing rule, minimum amount, validity dates, maximum use, and used count.](/images/docs/books-docs-coupon-code-1-coupon-form-v4.png)

1. Create a [pricing rule](/masters/pricing-rule/) and select **Is Coupon Code Based**. This rule applies only when the invoice has its coupon.
2. Click **Sales** > **Coupon Code** in the sidebar.
3. Click **New**.
4. Enter a **Name**, such as `Summer Sale`. RareBooks fills the **Coupon Code**, such as `SUMMERSA`. You can change the code before you save, but not after.
5. Select the **Pricing Rule**.
6. Set **Valid From** and **Valid To**.
7. In **Maximum Use**, enter the number of invoices that can use the coupon. Enter `0` for no limit.
8. Optional: Enter a **Min Amount** and a **Max Amount** for the order value.
9. Click **Save**.

The order value is the total of the rate multiplied by the quantity of all the rows, without discounts and taxes. Enter the amounts in your company currency.

The amounts and dates of the coupon must stay inside the limits of its pricing rule. **Used** shows the number of submitted invoices that use the coupon.

## Apply a Coupon on a Sales Invoice

1. Open a draft sales invoice.
2. Select the customer and add the items.
3. In **Coupons**, click **Add row**, and select the coupon code in the new row. On a phone, select it in the sheet that opens, and tap **Done**.
4. Click **Save**.

RareBooks applies the pricing rule of the coupon. The **Pricing Rule Detail** table shows the rule. You can add more than one coupon, but not the same coupon two times.

RareBooks shows an error and does not save the invoice when the coupon is disabled, used up, out of its dates or amounts, or its pricing rule does not apply to an item of the invoice.

:::tip[Priority]
If a different rule with a higher priority applies to the same item, the coupon rule does not apply, and RareBooks shows an error. Give the coupon-based rule a higher **Priority** than the other rules for the same items.
:::

When you submit an invoice, RareBooks adds 1 to **Used** for each coupon. If this makes **Used** more than **Maximum Use**, the invoice does not submit. When you cancel the invoice, RareBooks takes 1 away from **Used**.

## Apply a Coupon in the POS

1. Select the customer and add the items.
2. Click **Coupon** above the totals of the cart.
3. In **Coupons**, select the coupon code. If the coupon does not apply, the reason shows below the field.
4. Click **Done**.

On a phone, tap **Apply coupon code** on the payment screen.

The coupon does not apply when **Ignore Pricing Rule** is on in the POS settings or the POS profile. If you change the cart or the price list so that the coupon no longer applies, RareBooks removes it from the sale and shows a warning. See [Point of Sale (POS)](/transactions/point-of-sale/).
