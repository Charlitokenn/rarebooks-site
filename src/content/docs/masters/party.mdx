A party is a customer or a supplier. You sell to customers, and you buy from suppliers. A party can also be both a customer and a supplier.

A **Books User** can create, edit, and delete parties. You cannot delete a party that a document uses. See [Roles and Permissions](/getting-started/roles-and-permissions/).

## Party Lists

| List | Sidebar | Shows the Parties With the Role | A New Party Gets the Role |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Customers | **Sales** > **Customers** | **Customer** or **Both** | **Customer** |
| Suppliers | **Purchases** > **Suppliers** | **Supplier** or **Both** | **Supplier** |
| Party | **Common** > **Party** | All roles | **Both** |

The **Outstanding Amount** column shows the unpaid total of the submitted invoices, in the company currency. For a party with the role **Both**, it is the sales total minus the purchase total.

## Create a Party

1. Click **Sales** > **Customers** in the sidebar. For a supplier, click **Purchases** > **Suppliers**.
2. Click **New**.
3. Enter the **Name**. You cannot change it after you save.
4. Optional: Enter the **Email**, the **Phone**, and the [address](#add-an-address).
5. Click **Save**.

On the phone dashboard, you can also tap the **+** button in the header, and then tap **Customer**.

You can also type a new name in the **Customer** or **Supplier** field of an invoice and click **Create**. To make a customer from a lead, see [Lead](/transactions/lead/).

## Role

A **Customer** can be on sales quotes and sales invoices. A **Supplier** can be on purchase invoices. **Both** can be on all of them. If an invoice has a party with the wrong role, RareBooks does not save the invoice.

You can change the role after you save. RareBooks then clears the **Default Account**, and fills the account of the new role when you save.

## Party Fields

| Field | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Default Account | The receivable account of a customer or the payable account of a supplier. Invoices take their **Account** from it. If it is empty, RareBooks fills the **Default Receivable Account** or the **Default Payable Account** of the [settings](/settings/settings/#accounts) when you save. If that is also empty, it fills **Debtors** or **Creditors**, if your chart of accounts has them. |
| Currency | The currency of the invoices of the party. The default is the company currency. See [Multi Currency Invoicing](/transactions/multicurrency-accounting/). |
| Tax ID | The tax number of the party. It does not show for a company in India. |
| Loyalty Program | The loyalty program of a customer. Shows only when loyalty programs are on. See [Loyalty Program](/transactions/loyalty-program/). |
| Loyalty Points | Read only. The points that the customer can use now. |

A party with the role **Both** does not get a default account. You can select a receivable or a payable account. RareBooks uses it only on the invoices that need that type of account.

For a company in India, the **Billing** section has **GST Registration** instead of **Tax ID**. For **Registered Regular**, the **GSTIN No.** is required and must be a valid 15-character GSTIN. It shows on printed invoices.

## Add an Address

1. Open the party.
2. Click **Address**, and then click **Create**.
3. Enter **Address Line 1** and **City / Town**, and select the **Country**.
4. Click **Save**.
5. Click **Save** in the party form.

The address shows on printed invoices. To edit an address later, search for `Address` in [Quick Search](/getting-started/quick-search/). For a company in India, select the **State** from the list. RareBooks fills the **Place of Supply** from it.

## Party Actions

The **…** button in the page header has these actions:

![Customer Kaveri Enterprises with address, GST registration, and GSTIN, and the … menu open with Linked entries, Full width, Create sale, View sales, and Delete.](/images/docs/books-docs-party-1-customer-actions-v4.png)

- **Create sale** and **View sales**: Open a new sales invoice, or the sales invoices of the party. They show for **Customer** and **Both**.
- **Create purchase** and **View purchases**: The same for purchase invoices. They show for **Supplier** and **Both**.
- **Delete**: You cannot delete a party that a document uses.

**View** > **General Ledger** opens the [General Ledger](/financial-reports/general-ledger/) with the entries of the party. The link icon in the page header shows the linked invoices and payments. On a phone, all these actions are in the **…** menu in the header.
