Master data is the set of records that your transactions use, such as customers, items, and tax templates. A master does not make ledger entries. When you select a master in an invoice, RareBooks copies its values to the invoice.

### Masters Without a Sidebar Entry

Some masters do not have a sidebar entry, such as addresses, item groups, and units. You make them from the field that links to them, such as the **Address** field of a party. To open their list, search for the name in [Quick Search](/getting-started/quick-search/) and open the **List** result.

## Create a Master

Open the list in the sidebar, click **New**, fill in the fields, and click **Save**. The list fills some values for you. For example, a new entry in **Customers** gets the role **Customer**, and a new entry in **Sales Items** gets the purpose **Sales**.

### Create a Master From a Field

You can make a master while you fill in a different form, such as a customer while you make a sales invoice.

![New sales invoice with the quick edit panel open for a new customer, Sapphire Interiors, that was made from the Customer field.](/images/docs/books-docs-master-data-1-create-from-field-v4.png)

1. Click the field, such as **Customer**.
2. Type the name of the new entry.
3. Click **Create**. The quick edit panel opens next to the form.
4. Fill in the fields and click **Save**.

RareBooks selects the new entry in the field. The panel shows only the main fields. On a phone, the new entry opens in a sheet. **Create** shows only when you can create that type of entry.

## Duplicate or Delete a Master

To copy an entry, click **Create** > **Duplicate** in the page header. The name of the copy ends with `CPY`. To delete an entry, click **…** > **Delete**. On a phone, both actions are in the **…** menu in the header.

You cannot delete a master that a document uses. RareBooks shows an error with the name of the document. Only a **Books Manager** or a **System Manager** can change tax templates, accounts, and price lists.

## Import Masters

To add many masters at one time, use the [Import Wizard](/getting-started/import-wizard/). It can import parties, items, tax templates, accounts, addresses, and number series.
