Add some initial entries before you record transactions.

## Add Bank Accounts

The setup wizard makes one bank account. Add your other bank accounts in the Chart of Accounts.

1. Click **Setup** > **Chart of Accounts** in the sidebar.
2. Open **Application of Funds (Assets)** > **Current Assets**.
3. Move the pointer over **Bank Accounts** and click the **…** button.
4. Click **Add account**.
5. Enter the name of the bank account and click **Save**.

You can now use this account for payments.

:::note
The Chart of Accounts page is not available on a phone. Use a computer for these steps.
:::

## Add Taxes

If your company charges sales tax or pays purchase tax, add a tax template for each tax rate.

1. Click **Setup** > **Tax Templates** in the sidebar.
2. Click **New**.
3. Enter a **Name** that shows the tax and its rate, such as `GST 18%`.
4. In **Details**, add a row for each tax account:
   - **Tax Invoice Account**: The account that gets the tax when you submit an invoice.
   - **Tax Payment Account**: Optional. When a payment is made for the invoice, the tax moves to this account.
   - **Rate**: The tax rate in percent.
5. Click **Save**.

A template can have more than one row. Use this for taxes that you always charge together. For more information, see [Taxes](/masters/taxes/).

## Add Sales Items

Add the products and services that you sell.

![New sales item that is not saved, with item name, item code, item group, rate 199, and tax GST-12.](/images/docs/books-docs-create-initial-entries-1-new-sales-item-v4.png)

1. Click **Sales** > **Sales Items** in the sidebar.
2. Click **New**.
3. Enter the **Item Name** and the **Rate**.
4. Set **Type** to **Product** or **Service**.
5. In **Tax**, select a tax template. RareBooks adds this tax when you add the item to an invoice.
6. Click **Save**.

You can also set an image, the unit, and the accounts of the item. **Sales Acc.** is the income account, and **Purchase Acc.** is the expense account. For more information, see [Items](/masters/items/).

## Add Customers

Add your customers so that you can select them in sales invoices.

1. Click **Sales** > **Customers** in the sidebar.
2. Click **New**.
3. Enter the **Name** of the customer.
4. Click **Save**.

You can also add the **Email**, the **Phone**, and the **Address** of the customer. The address shows on printed sales invoices. For more information, see [Party](/masters/party/).
