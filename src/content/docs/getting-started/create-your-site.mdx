RareBooks runs on your own site, such as `yourcompany.rarebooks.cc`. You and your team sign in to that site from a browser on a computer or a phone.

All company data stays on your site. There is nothing to install and no file to keep.

## Sign Up

1. On [rarebooks.cc](https://rarebooks.cc), click **Start free trial**.
2. Create your account and verify your email.
3. In **Your address**, type the name for your site, such as `yourcompany`. Wait until the field shows **Available**.
4. Enter the **Company name**.
5. Click **Create my site**.

Each account can create one site.

## Wait for Your Site

Your dashboard shows the status of the site: **Getting your site**, then **Setting up your address**, then **Ready**. This usually takes a few minutes. You can close the page. We email you when your site is ready.

Your free trial starts on the day you create the site and lasts 30 days.

## Open Your Site

1. On your dashboard, click **Set your password**.
2. Enter a password for your user on the site.

The setup wizard opens on the first sign-in, with your company name filled in. To complete it, see [Getting Started with RareBooks](/getting-started/getting-started-with-rarebooks/).

The user that created the site is the owner. It has the **System Manager** and the **Books Manager** roles. To add your team, see [Roles and Permissions](/getting-started/roles-and-permissions/).

:::note
To open your site later, go to `https://<your-site>/books`, or click **Set your password** on your dashboard. If you have a problem with your site, write to [support@rarebooks.cc](mailto:support@rarebooks.cc).
:::
