The chart of accounts is the list of all the accounts of your company. RareBooks makes it from the chart that you select in the setup wizard.

To open it, click **Setup** > **Chart of Accounts** in the sidebar. This page is not available on a phone.

## Organization of Accounts

There are two types of accounts:

1. **Ledger accounts**: Transactions use these accounts. They show in the General Ledger. A ledger account has a circle icon and shows its balance, such as `1,000.00 Dr.`
2. **Group accounts**: These accounts contain other group accounts and ledger accounts. A group account has a folder icon. Some root accounts have a different icon.

### Root Accounts

The accounts at the top level are the root accounts. The Standard Chart of Accounts has five root accounts: **Application of Funds (Assets)**, **Source of Funds (Liabilities)**, **Equity**, **Income**, and **Expenses**. The charts of other countries can have a different number of root accounts.

Each root account is a debit account or a credit account. All the accounts below a credit root account are also credit accounts. For more information, see [Credit and Debit](/basics/accounting-basics/#credit-and-debit).

### Open the Groups

All the groups are closed when the page opens. Click a group, or the arrow before it, to open or close it. Click **Expand** or **Collapse** at the top of the page to open or close all the groups.

### View an Account

Click a ledger account to open it in a side panel. The panel shows these fields:

- **Root Type**. It is read only when the account has a **Parent Account**.
- **Parent Account**
- **Account Type**
- **Is Group**
- **GST Head**, only for a company in India. The GST reports use it to find the GST amounts. Select **CGST**, **SGST**, **IGST**, or **Exempt**. For more information, see [GST Reports](/financial-reports/gst-reports/#gst-accounts-and-tax-templates).

To change a value, edit the field and click **Save**. To close the panel, click the **X** button.

## Create a New Account

1. Move the pointer over a group account.
2. Click the **…** button at the end of the row.
3. Click **Add account** or **Add group**.
4. Enter the account name.
5. Click **Save**, or press `Enter`.

![Chart of Accounts with the asset and income groups open and the ledger balances, and the menu of the Bank Accounts group with Add account, Add group, and Delete group.](/images/docs/books-docs-chart-of-accounts-1-add-account-menu-v4.png)

The new account gets the root type and the account type of its group. RareBooks then opens the account in the side panel, where you can change the account type.

To make a new root account, click **Add root group** at the top of the page. Enter the account name and the root type in the side panel. Then click **Save**.

## Delete an Account

1. Move the pointer over the account.
2. Click the **…** button on the right side of the row.
3. Click **Delete account** or **Delete group**.
4. Click **Delete** to confirm. To stop, click **Keep Account**, or **Keep Group** for a group.

You cannot undo a delete. You cannot delete a root account, or a group that contains other accounts.
