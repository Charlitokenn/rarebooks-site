RareBooks is an accounting app for small businesses. It runs on your own RareBooks site, such as `yourcompany.rarebooks.cc`, and you use it in a browser on a computer or a phone. If you do not have a site yet, see [Create Your Site](/getting-started/create-your-site/).

## Open RareBooks

1. Open `https://<your-site>/books` in a browser.
2. If you are not signed in, sign in with your email and password.

Your user must have a Books role to open RareBooks. For more information, see [Roles and Permissions](/getting-started/roles-and-permissions/).

## Set Up Your Organization

The setup wizard opens the first time a **System Manager** opens RareBooks on a new site. Only a System Manager can complete it, and the setup runs only once. Until then, other users cannot use RareBooks.

![Setup wizard on a new site, filled in for Lotus Traders in India with a logo, INR, Standard Chart of Accounts, and the fiscal year April 2026 to March 2027.](/images/docs/books-docs-getting-started-with-frappe-books-1-setup-wizard.png)

1. Enter the details of your company.
2. Click **Submit**.

On a phone, the wizard has three steps. Tap **Next** to go to the next step, and tap **Submit** on the last step.

| Field | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Company Logo and Email | They show on printed documents. The logo is optional. |
| Country | RareBooks uses the country to fill the currency, the chart of accounts, and the fiscal year. |
| Bank Name | RareBooks makes a bank account with this name. |
| Chart of Accounts | The list of accounts for your country. If there is no chart for your country, select **Standard Chart of Accounts**. |
| Fiscal Year Start Date and Fiscal Year End Date | The first and the last day of your financial year. RareBooks fills these dates for some countries only. For other countries, enter the start date, and RareBooks fills the end date. A fiscal year cannot be longer than one year. |

After you submit, RareBooks makes the chart of accounts, the bank account, a **Cash** account, and the default settings. Then the **Dashboard** opens. The setup does not turn on automatic payments of invoices. To turn them on, see [Auto Payments](/settings/settings/#auto-payments).

## Get Started Page

The **Get Started** page lists the tasks that prepare your company for use. To open it, click **Get Started** in the sidebar. This page is not available on a phone.

![Get Started page with the title Set up your workspace. Each task card shows its Set up and Documentation buttons, and the complete tasks have green checks.](/images/docs/books-docs-getting-started-with-frappe-books-2-get-started-page-v4.png)

Click **Set up** on a task to open its page, or **Documentation** to open its help page. Some tasks have only one of these buttons. A complete task shows a green check instead. When all the tasks are complete, RareBooks removes **Get Started** from the sidebar.

## Save and Submit Documents

A transaction, such as an invoice or a payment, goes through these states:

| State | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Draft | You saved the document. You can still change it. |
| Submitted | RareBooks made the ledger entries. You cannot change the document. |
| Cancelled | RareBooks reversed the ledger entries. You cannot change the document. |

On a computer, the main button is in the page header. It shows **Save** while the document has changes that are not saved. After you save, it shows **Submit**. RareBooks asks you to confirm each save and submit. Click **Yes** to continue. On a phone, the main button is at the bottom of the form, and RareBooks asks you to confirm only when you submit.

To cancel a submitted document, click the **…** button in the page header, and then click **Cancel**. To confirm, click **Cancel** and the document type, such as **Cancel Sales Invoice**. To delete a document, click **…** > **Delete**, and then click **Delete** to confirm. In both confirmations, the other button keeps the document, such as **Keep Sales Invoice**.
