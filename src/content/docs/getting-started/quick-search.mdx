Quick Search finds documents, lists, reports, and pages in RareBooks. It also opens new forms.

## Open Quick Search

- On a computer, click the search icon in the page header. You can also press `Ctrl + K`, or `Cmd + K` on macOS.
- On a phone, tap **Search** in the bottom tabs.

![Phone Search tab with the results for "kaveri": the party, its shipments, and its payments.](/images/docs/books-docs-quick-search-2-phone-search-v4.png)

## Search

Type in the search box. Uppercase and lowercase letters are the same.

For a list, a form, a report, or a page, type some letters of its name in the same order. For example, `sinv` finds **Sales Invoice**.

Quick Search finds a document by its name, its document type, its main fields, and the main fields of its table rows. For example, it finds an invoice by its number, its customer, or an item in it. It finds documents by words:

- Each word that you type must match. The words can be in any order. For example, `karen invoice` finds the invoices of the customer Karen.
- A word of four or more characters also finds the words that start with it. For example, `kare` finds `Karen`. A shorter word must match a full word.
- A hyphen separates words. For example, `1001` finds **SINV-1001**.

A new or changed document can take some minutes to show in the results.

Quick Search shows only the documents, lists, and reports that you can read. It shows a **Create** result only for documents that you can create. It does not show the lists of features that are off.

### Results

Each result has a gray label at the right that shows its type.

| Label | Opens |
| --- | --- |
| The document type, such as **Sales Invoice** | The document |
| **List** | The list view |
| **Create** | A new form |
| **Report** | The report |
| **Page** | The page |
| **Recent** | An item that you opened recently |

Lists, forms, reports, and pages show before documents. A document ranks higher when your words are in its document type or its name, and when it changed recently. Cancelled documents show last.

Quick Search shows the first 50 results. To show more, select **100**, **500**, or **All** at the bottom. On a phone, tap **Show all**.

When the search box is empty, Quick Search shows the last 10 items that you opened. To search them, start with `#`, such as `#cust`.

## Filters

To hide a result type, click its button at the bottom of the dialog: **Docs**, **List**, **Create**, **Report**, **Page**, or **Recent**. Click it again to show the type.

![Quick Search for "sales invoice" with List, Create, and document results, and the extra filters open with Skip transactions and the document type buttons.](/images/docs/books-docs-quick-search-1-results-and-filters-v4.png)

To hide document types, click **More filters**. **Skip transactions** hides all transactions, such as invoices and payments. Click a document type to hide it, and click it again to show it. A filled button shows that its type is in the results.

On a phone:

1. To hide a result type, tap its button below the search box.
2. Tap **Filters**.
3. In the **Search filters** sheet, tap **Skip transactions** or each document type that you want to hide.
4. Tap **Done**.

The filters stay set until you reload the page.

## Keyboard Shortcuts

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| `Up` and `Down` | Select the previous or next result |
| `Enter` | Open the selected result |
| `Esc` | Close Quick Search |
| `Ctrl + 1` to `Ctrl + 6` | Hide or show a result type: **Docs**, **List**, **Create**, **Report**, **Page**, or **Recent** |

On macOS, press `Cmd` instead of `Ctrl`. Click **Help** at the bottom of the dialog to open this page.
