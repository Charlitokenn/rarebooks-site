If you move from a different accounting system, enter the account balances from that system in RareBooks. You do this with one journal entry of the type **Opening Entry**.

## Get the Starting Balances

Get a Trial Balance report from your previous accounting system. Use the balances on the day before you start to use RareBooks.

## Add the Missing Accounts

Some accounts from your previous system can be missing in RareBooks. Add them in the Chart of Accounts. For the steps, see [Create a New Account](/getting-started/chart-of-accounts/#create-a-new-account).

Make sure that you add each account below the correct group.

## Make an Opening Entry

1. Click **Common** > **Journal Entry** in the sidebar.
2. Click **New**.
3. Set **Entry Type** to **Opening Entry**.
4. Set **Date** to the date of the balances.
5. In **Account Entries**, add one row for each account in your Trial Balance report.
6. In each row, select the **Account** and enter the **Debit** or the **Credit** amount.
7. Click **Save**.
8. Check the entry. Then click **Submit** and click **Yes**.

![Submitted opening entry with debits to HDFC Bank, Cash, and Stock Adjustment, credits to Secured Loans and Capital Stock, and a reference number.](/images/docs/books-docs-setup-opening-balances-1-opening-entry-v4.png)

The total of the debits must be equal to the total of the credits. If the totals are not equal, add a row with the **Opening Balance Equity** account. RareBooks fills the new row with the amount that balances the entry. In the Standard Chart of Accounts, this account is in **Equity**.

:::tip[References]
You can enter a **Reference Number** and a **Reference Date** in the **References** section. Use them to find the entry later.
:::

After you submit the entry, your accounts show the same balances as your previous system. You can now record your transactions.
