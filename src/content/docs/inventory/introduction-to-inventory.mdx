Inventory keeps track of the stock of your items. RareBooks records each movement of stock into a location, out of a location, or between two locations. It also calculates the value of your stock and records this value in your accounts.

## Turn On Inventory

Only a **Books Manager** or a **System Manager** can change the settings.

![Inventory group in the sidebar with Stock Movement, Shipment, and Purchase Receipt, and the list of stock movements.](/images/docs/books-docs-introduction-to-inventory-1-inventory-menu-v4.png)

1. Click **Setup** > **Settings** in the sidebar.
2. In the **General** tab, turn on **Enable Inventory**.
3. Click **Save**.
4. Click **Yes** to reload RareBooks.

:::caution
You cannot turn off inventory after you turn it on.
:::

The sidebar then shows the **Inventory** group and the stock reports. You can also turn on the [Point of Sale (POS)](/transactions/point-of-sale/).

| Page | Use |
| --- | --- |
| [Stock Movement](/inventory/stock-movement/) | Moves stock into, out of, or between locations. |
| [Shipment](/inventory/shipment/) | Records stock that goes to a customer. |
| [Purchase Receipt](/inventory/purchase-receipt/) | Records stock that comes from a supplier. |
| [Stock Ledger](/inventory/stock-ledger/) | Shows each stock entry. |
| [Stock Balance](/inventory/stock-balance/) | Shows the stock of each item at each location. |

A sales invoice or a purchase invoice can make its shipment or purchase receipt when you submit it. A **Books User** can make, submit, and cancel stock movements, shipments, and purchase receipts. Only a **Books Manager** or a **System Manager** can delete them.

## Track an Item

1. Make a new item. For the steps, see [Items](/masters/items/).
2. Set **Type** to **Product**.
3. In the **Inventory** section, select **Track Inventory**.
4. Click **Save**.

You can select **Track Inventory** only on a new product, and you cannot change it after you save. Stock movements, shipments, and purchase receipts accept only these items. Their **Purchase Acc.** must be a liability account, and RareBooks fills it with the **Stock Received But Not Billed Acc.**

## Locations

Stock is always at a location. RareBooks has a location called **Stores**, which is the [Default Location](/inventory/inventory-settings/#default-location). To add a location, type its name in a location field and click **Create**. Only a **Books Manager** or a **System Manager** can make locations. To see all your locations, search for `Location` in [Quick Search](/getting-started/quick-search/).

## Stock Quantity

An entry that takes stock out of a location needs enough stock there, on its date and on all later dates. The same check applies when you cancel an entry that brought stock in. If the stock is not enough, RareBooks shows the available and the required quantity. Select a location that has enough stock, or bring the stock in first with an earlier date. To find a location, see [Stock Balance](/inventory/stock-balance/).

## Stock Valuation

RareBooks values stock with the FIFO (first in, first out) method. Stock that comes in adds a layer at its rate. Stock that goes out takes the value of the oldest layers first, not the rate of its row. Each item, location, and batch has its own layers.

For example, you receive an item two times and then ship 15 units:

| Entry | Quantity | Rate | Value of the Entry | Stock Value After the Entry |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Purchase receipt | 10 | 100 | 1,000 | 1,000 |
| Purchase receipt | 10 | 120 | 1,200 | 2,200 |
| Shipment | -15 | | -1,600 | 600 |

The shipment takes 10 units at 100 and 5 units at 120. The 5 units that stay have a value of 600.

A **Material Transfer** and a shipment return bring the stock in at the value that went out.

When you submit or cancel an entry with an earlier date, RareBooks calculates the later entries again and updates their accounting entries.

## Stock Accounts

RareBooks posts the change in the stock value to the accounts of the [inventory settings](/inventory/inventory-settings/#accounts). If one of the accounts that a document needs is empty, you cannot submit the document.

| Document | Debit | Credit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Purchase receipt | Stock In Hand | Stock Received But Not Billed |
| Purchase invoice for a tracked item | Stock Received But Not Billed | The payable account, such as Creditors |
| Shipment | Cost of Goods Sold | Stock In Hand |
| Stock movement that adds stock value, such as a **Material Receipt** | Stock In Hand | Stock Adjustment |
| Stock movement that removes stock value, such as a **Material Issue** | Stock Adjustment | Stock In Hand |

The purchase invoice and the purchase receipt cancel each other in **Stock Received But Not Billed**, so the stock value stays in **Stock In Hand**. A shipment then moves the value to **Cost of Goods Sold**. A **Material Transfer** makes no accounting entries.

## Next Steps

1. Check the accounts and the default location in [Inventory Settings](/inventory/inventory-settings/).
2. Make your products with **Track Inventory** selected.
3. Record the stock that you have now with a [stock movement](/inventory/stock-movement/) of the type **Material Receipt**.

:::note[Opening Stock Value]
A **Material Receipt** credits the **Stock Adjustment Acc.** So in your [opening entry](/getting-started/setup-opening-balances/), put the stock value on the **Stock Adjustment Acc.**, not on the **Stock In Hand Acc.** The two entries then cancel each other.
:::
