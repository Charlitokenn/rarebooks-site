The inventory settings set the default location, the stock accounts, and the inventory features that you use. The **Inventory** tab of the settings shows only when inventory is on. To turn on inventory, see [Introduction to Inventory](/inventory/introduction-to-inventory/#turn-on-inventory).

## Open the Inventory Settings

1. Click **Setup** > **Settings** in the sidebar.
2. Click **Inventory**.
3. Change the settings.
4. Click **Save**.
5. Click **Yes** to reload RareBooks. Your changes show after the reload.

![Inventory tab of the settings with the default location Stores, the four stock accounts, and the feature switches. Enable Batches and Enable Serial Number are on and locked.](/images/docs/books-docs-inventory-settings-1-inventory-tab-v4.png)

Only a **Books Manager** or a **System Manager** can change the settings. A **Books User** can only read them.

## Default Location

**Default Location** is the location that RareBooks puts in a stock row when the row has no location. It is used for:

- The **From** location of a **Material Issue** and the **To** location of a **Material Receipt**. See [Stock Movement](/inventory/stock-movement/).
- A shipment row or a purchase receipt row that you leave empty.

The setup wizard sets it to **Stores**. To add a location, type its name in the field and click **Create**. Sales invoices and purchase invoices use their own locations. See [Related Settings](#related-settings).

## Accounts

RareBooks posts the value of the stock to these accounts when you submit a shipment, a purchase receipt, or a stock movement. The setup wizard fills them from your chart of accounts.

| Field | Account Type | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Stock In Hand Acc. | Stock | The value of the stock that you have. |
| Stock Received But Not Billed Acc. | Stock Received But Not Billed | The value of the stock that you received but did not bill yet. |
| Cost Of Goods Sold Acc. | Cost of Goods Sold | The cost of the stock that you shipped. |
| Stock Adjustment Acc. | Stock Adjustment | The other side of a stock movement, such as a **Material Receipt**. |

Each list shows only ledger accounts of the account type in the table. To add an account, see [Create a New Account](/getting-started/chart-of-accounts/#create-a-new-account). For the entries that each document makes, see [Stock Accounts](/inventory/introduction-to-inventory/#stock-accounts).

:::note
If an account that a document needs is empty, you cannot submit the document.
:::

RareBooks values stock with the FIFO (first in, first out) method. You cannot select a different method. See [Stock Valuation](/inventory/introduction-to-inventory/#stock-valuation).

## Features

| Field | What It Does |
| --- | --- |
| Enable Barcodes | Adds a **Barcode** field to items, and above the item rows of draft forms. Type or scan a barcode to add the item. A barcode has exactly 12 digits. On a phone, tap the scan button to scan with the camera. |
| Enable Batches | Adds **Has Batch** to items that track inventory, and a **Batch** field to item rows. See [Batches](/inventory/batches/). |
| Enable Serial Number | Adds **Has Serial Number** to items, and a **Serial Number** field to item rows. See [Serial Number](/inventory/serial-number/). |
| Enable UOM Conversion | Adds a **UOM Conversions** table to items. Item rows get a **Transfer Unit** and a **Qty. in Transfer Unit**. |
| Enable Point of Sale | Shows **POS** in the sidebar and the **POS Settings** tab. See [Point of Sale](/transactions/point-of-sale/). |

After you save, you cannot turn off barcodes, batches, serial numbers, or UOM conversion. You can turn off **Enable Point of Sale**, but not while a POS shift is open.

:::caution[POS Without Inventory]
**Enable POS Without Inventory** in the **General** tab also turns on all the options in **Features**. You cannot turn off the first four options after that.
:::

To return shipments and purchase receipts, turn on **Enable Invoice Returns** in the **General** tab.

## Related Settings

These settings are in the **Defaults** tab:

| Field | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Shipment Location | The location that a sales invoice ships from. While it is empty, only POS sales make shipments on submit. See [Automatic Shipments](/transactions/sales-invoice/#automatic-shipments). |
| Purchase Receipt Location | The location that a purchase invoice receives into. While it is empty, purchase invoices do not make purchase receipts on submit. See [Purchase Receipts](/transactions/purchase-invoice/#purchase-receipts). |
| Number series and print templates | The default number series and the print template that opens first for stock movements, shipments, and purchase receipts. See [Number Series](/settings/number-series/) and [Print Templates](/settings/print-templates/). |
| Shipment Terms, Purchase Receipt Terms | The text that new shipments and purchase receipts get in **Notes**. |
