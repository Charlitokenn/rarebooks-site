A batch is a group of units of an item that you make or buy together, such as one production lot. When an item has batches, RareBooks keeps the stock and the value of each batch separately. Each document row of the item names its batch.

## Turn On Batches

Batches need [inventory](/inventory/introduction-to-inventory/#turn-on-inventory). Turn on **Enable Batches** in the **Features** section of the [inventory settings](/inventory/inventory-settings/#features). Then save the settings and reload RareBooks.

:::caution
You cannot turn off batches after you turn them on.
:::

## Set Up an Item

1. Open the item, for example from **Sales** > **Sales Items** in the sidebar.
2. Make sure that **Track Inventory** is selected.
3. Select **Has Batch**.
4. Optional: Enter a **Batch Series**, such as `LOT`.
5. Click **Save**.

**Has Batch** shows only when **Track Inventory** is selected. You can select **Track Inventory** only on a new [item](/masters/items/).

### Batch Series

RareBooks makes a new batch for each row of the item that has no batch. It does this when you save a purchase invoice, a purchase receipt, or a **Material Receipt**. This does not apply to returns. If an item has no batch series, select or make a batch in each row.

RareBooks adds a dash and a number to the series, starting at 1001. For example, the series `LOT` gives `LOT-1001`, `LOT-1002`, and so on. It skips the numbers that are already used. A series cannot contain `/`, `?`, `&`, `=`, or `%`.

## Make a Batch

There is no sidebar item for batches. Search for `Batch` in [Quick Search](/getting-started/quick-search/) and open the **List** result.

1. In the list of batches, click **New**.
2. Enter the name of the batch in **Batch**.
3. Select the **Item**.
4. Optional: Enter the **Expiry Date** and the **Manufacture Date**. The expiry date cannot be before the manufacture date.
5. Click **Save**.

You can also make a batch from the row of a stock movement, an invoice, or a quote. Type the name of the new batch in **Batch** and click **Create**. The new batch gets the item of the row.

You cannot change the item of a batch after you save it.

RareBooks does not check the expiry date. For example, you can sell a batch after its expiry date.

## Select a Batch in a Document

1. Add the item to the document.
2. Click the **Edit row** button at the end of the row. On a phone, tap the row.
3. In **Batch**, select the batch.

![Row panel of a draft sales invoice with HSN/SAC 0902 and the Batch list open, which shows three batches of Darjeeling Tea.](/images/docs/books-docs-batches-1-select-batch-v4.png)

In a sales invoice or a sales quote, the list shows only the batches that have stock at the location that the sale ships from. Other documents and returns show all the batches of the item. In the [Point of Sale](/transactions/point-of-sale/), the **Select Batch** dialog opens when you add an item with batches.

A sale ships from the **Shipment Location** in the **Defaults** settings, or from the POS inventory for a POS sale. If the **Shipment Location** is empty, RareBooks uses the stock of all locations.

On a sales invoice or a sales quote, RareBooks checks the stock of the batch. It checks when you select the batch, when you change **Qty**, and when you save. If the batch does not have enough stock, the field shows an error. It does not check the batch stock on a return.

## Rules for Batches

RareBooks shows an error and does not save or submit the document in these cases:

- A row of an item with batches has no batch.
- A row of an item without batches has a batch.
- The batch belongs to a different item.
- When you submit a document that takes stock out, the batch does not have enough stock at the source location.

## Batches in Reports

When batches are on, the [Stock Ledger](/inventory/stock-ledger/) and the [Stock Balance](/inventory/stock-balance/) have a **Batch** column and a **Batch** filter. The Stock Balance shows one row for each batch of an item at a location.
