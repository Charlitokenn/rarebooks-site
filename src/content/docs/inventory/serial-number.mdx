A serial number identifies one unit of an item. When an item has serial numbers, RareBooks keeps a Serial Number record for each unit. The status of the record shows if the unit is in stock.

## Turn On Serial Numbers

Serial numbers need [inventory](/inventory/introduction-to-inventory/#turn-on-inventory). Turn on **Enable Serial Number** in the **Features** section of the [inventory settings](/inventory/inventory-settings/#features). Then save the settings and reload RareBooks.

:::caution
You cannot turn off serial numbers after you turn them on.
:::

## Set Up an Item

1. Open the item, for example from **Sales** > **Sales Items** in the sidebar.
2. Make sure that **Track Inventory** is selected.
3. Select **Has Serial Number**.
4. Optional: Enter a **Serial Number Series**, such as `SN`.
5. Click **Save**.

![Inventory section of Thermal Receipt Printer with Track Inventory, Has Serial Number, and the serial number series TRP-.](/images/docs/books-docs-serial-number-1-item-setup-v4.png)

**Has Serial Number** shows only when **Track Inventory** is selected. You can select **Track Inventory** only on a new [item](/masters/items/).

### Serial Number Series

When you save a purchase receipt or a **Material Receipt**, RareBooks adds the missing serial numbers of the item from its series. This also applies to the purchase receipt that a purchase invoice makes.

RareBooks adds a dash and a number to the series, starting at 1001. For example, the series `SN` gives `SN-1001`, `SN-1002`, and so on. It skips the numbers that are already used. A series cannot contain `/`, `?`, `&`, `=`, or `%`.

## Enter Serial Numbers

Each row of an item with serial numbers needs one serial number for each unit.

1. Add the item to the document and enter the quantity.
2. Click the **Edit row** button at the end of the row. On a phone, tap the row.
3. In **Serial Number**, enter one serial number on each line. You can also separate them with commas.

RareBooks also fills the serial numbers in these documents:

| Document | What RareBooks Does |
| --- | --- |
| Shipment | Fills the serial numbers that came in first at the location of the row. |
| Sales invoice, purchase invoice | Passes the serial numbers to its shipment or purchase receipt. If **Serial Number** is empty, that document fills them. |
| Point of Sale | Fills the serial numbers from the stock of the POS inventory. |

In other stock movements, enter the serial numbers yourself. If you change the quantity later, check the serial numbers again.

## The Serial Number List

There is no sidebar item for serial numbers. Search for `Serial Number` in [Quick Search](/getting-started/quick-search/) and open the **List** result.

RareBooks makes a serial number when a new one comes into stock, so you do not have to make them first. To make one before you use it, click **New** in the list, and enter the **Serial Number** and the **Item**. You cannot change the item after you save.

You cannot delete a serial number that a document uses.

## Serial Number Status

You cannot change the status yourself. RareBooks changes it when you submit a document:

| Status | Meaning | Set By |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Inactive | The unit is not in stock. A serial number that you make by hand starts with this status. | A **Material Issue**, the raw materials of a **Manufacture**, or a return of a purchase receipt |
| Active | The unit is in stock. | A purchase receipt, a **Material Receipt** or **Material Transfer**, the finished goods of a **Manufacture**, or a return of a shipment |
| Delivered | A shipment sent the unit to a customer. | A shipment |

When you cancel a document that took the unit out, the status changes to **Active**. When you cancel a document that only brought the unit in, it changes to **Inactive**. When you cancel a return of a shipment, it changes back to **Delivered**.

## Rules for Serial Numbers

RareBooks shows an error and does not save or submit the document in these cases:

- The number of serial numbers is not equal to the quantity.
- A serial number belongs to a different item, or shows more than one time in the document.
- A serial number that goes out is not in stock at the source location.
- A serial number that comes in is already in stock.
- On a return, a serial number is not on the original document, or is returned already.

You also cannot cancel a document that brought a unit into stock after the unit went out again.

## Serial Numbers in Reports

The [Stock Ledger](/inventory/stock-ledger/) has a **Serial Number** column with one row for each serial number. The [Stock Balance](/inventory/stock-balance/) has a **Serial Number** filter that shows the stock of each serial number.
