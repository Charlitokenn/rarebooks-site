The dashboard shows a summary of your invoices, your cash flow, your profit, and your expenses.

![Dashboard for This year with the four invoice cards, the Cashflow chart, the Profit and Loss chart, and the Top expenses chart with Total spending: ₹ 4,54,537.00 below its title.](/images/docs/books-docs-dashboard-1-overview-v5.png)

To open it, click **Dashboard** in the sidebar. On a phone, tap **Dashboard** in the bottom tabs.

The dashboard has four invoice cards at the top. Below them are the **Cashflow** chart, the **Profit and Loss** chart, and the **Top expenses** chart.

You see only the cards for the invoices that you can read. Only a **Books Manager** or a **System Manager** sees the charts. See [Roles and Permissions](/getting-started/roles-and-permissions/).

## Period

All the sections use the same period. To change it, select a period in the page header. On a phone, tap the period button above the cards, and then tap a period.

Each period ends today:

| Period | Starts On |
| --- | --- |
| **This year** | The first day of the month 11 months before the current month. This is the default. |
| **This quarter** | The first day of the month 2 months before the current month |
| **This month** | The first day of the current month |
| **Year to date** | The first day of the current fiscal year. See [Fiscal Year](/settings/settings/#fiscal-year). |

For example, on 2 October 2026, **This year** starts on 1 November 2025.

## Invoice Cards

| Card | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Paid sales** | The grand total of the sales invoices of the period that are fully paid |
| **Unpaid sales** | The amount that customers must still pay on the other sales invoices of the period |
| **Paid purchases** | The grand total of the purchase invoices of the period that are fully paid |
| **Unpaid purchases** | The amount that you must still pay on the other purchase invoices of the period |

The cards use only the submitted invoices with an invoice date in the period. A partly paid invoice adds only its outstanding amount to the unpaid card. Returns have negative amounts, so they decrease the amounts.

To see the invoices of a card, click the arrow button on the card. On a phone, tap the card.

## Charts

| Chart | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Cashflow** | The **Inflow** and the **Outflow** of each month, for all the accounts with the account type **Cash** or **Bank** |
| **Profit and Loss** | The income minus the expenses of each month. A month with a loss shows as a **Loss** bar below the zero line. |
| **Top expenses** | The five expense accounts with the highest amounts in the period. If more accounts have an amount, their total shows as one **Others** slice. The center shows a short total. The full **Total spending** shows under the chart title. |

To see the amounts of a month in the **Cashflow** or the **Profit and Loss** chart, move the pointer over the month. On a phone, tap the month. For the full statement, see [Profit and Loss Statement](/financial-reports/profit-and-loss-statement/).

## Create Entries on a Phone

1. Tap the **+** button in the header.
2. Tap the type of entry: **Sales Invoice**, **Receive payment**, **Purchase Invoice**, **Make payment**, **Customer**, or **Item**.

![Phone dashboard with the + menu open: Sales Invoice, Receive payment, Purchase Invoice, Make payment, Customer, and Item.](/images/docs/books-docs-dashboard-2-phone-create-menu-v5.png)

The list shows only the types that you can create.
