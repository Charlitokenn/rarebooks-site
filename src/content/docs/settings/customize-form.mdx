Customize Form adds your own fields to the forms of RareBooks. For example, add a **Delivery Date** to sales invoices or a **Color** to items.

Only a **System Manager** can customize forms. All users see the custom fields, and can fill them in on the documents that they can edit. See [Roles and Permissions](/getting-started/roles-and-permissions/).

:::note
The Customize Form page is not available on a phone. The custom fields also show on the forms on a phone.
:::

## Turn On Form Customization

Form customization is off in a new company.

1. Click **Setup** > **Settings** in the sidebar.
2. In **General** > **Features**, turn on **Enable Form Customization**.
3. Click **Save**, and then click **Yes** to reload RareBooks.

**Customize Form** then shows in the **Setup** group of the sidebar. If you turn it off later, the custom fields stay on the forms, but you cannot change them.

## Add Custom Fields

1. Click **Setup** > **Customize Form** in the sidebar.
2. Click **New**.
3. In **Form Type**, select the form that gets the fields, such as **Item** or **Sales Invoice**.
4. In **Custom Fields**, click **Add row**.
5. Enter the **Label** and select the **Fieldtype**. RareBooks fills the **Fieldname**.
6. To set more values, such as **Options**, click the **Edit row** button at the end of the row.
7. Add a row for each field, and then click **Save**.

![New custom form for Sales Invoice with two custom fields: Delivery Date of type Date and Sales Person of type Data.](/images/docs/books-docs-customize-form-1-custom-fields-v4.png)

The fields show after you save. Each form type has one custom form. To add more fields later, open it from the list. **Form Type** also lists table rows, such as **Sales Invoice Item** for the item rows of a sales invoice. You cannot customize the settings or the ledger entries.

## Custom Field Values

| Value | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Fieldname | The internal name, such as `deliveryDate` for `Delivery Date`. RareBooks makes it from the label only once. It must be unique in the form. |
| Fieldtype | **Data**, **Text**, **Int**, **Float**, **Currency**, **Check**, **Date**, **Date Time**, **Select**, **Autocomplete**, **Color**, **Link**, **Dynamic Link**, **Attach Image**, **Attachment**, or **Table**. |
| Is Required | Users must fill in the field before they save. A required field needs a **Default**. |
| Options | Enter each option on a new line. A **Select** or **Autocomplete** field needs at least two. A **Color** field can have its own palette, such as `#f98080`. |
| Target | For **Link** and **Table** fields. The form type that the field links to. A **Table** needs a table row type. |
| References | For **Dynamic Link** fields. A **Select** field of the same form. Its options must be internal form type names, such as `Books Party`. |
| Form Tab | The default `Custom` adds a **Custom** tab. Enter `Main` for the first tab, or a new name for a new tab. |
| Form Section | The title of a section in the tab, or a new title for a new section at the end. The default `Default` is the first section. |

Custom fields show after the other fields of their tab and section, in the order of the rows.

## Where Custom Fields Show

- **Table rows**: In the row panel. Click the **Edit row** button at the end of the row. On a phone, tap the row.
- **Side panel**: When you create a record from a link field, the panel shows only the required custom fields.
- **Export and import**: List exports and the [Import Wizard](/getting-started/import-wizard/) include the custom fields.
- **Print templates**: The standard templates do not show custom fields. In your own [template](/settings/print-templates/), the key is `doc.custom_books_` and the fieldname in lowercase, such as `doc.custom_books_deliverydate`.

## Change or Remove Custom Fields

1. Click **Setup** > **Customize Form** in the sidebar.
2. Click the form type.
3. Change the rows. To remove a field, move the pointer over the row number and click the **x** button.
4. Click **Save**.

To remove all the custom fields of a form type, click **…** > **Delete** in its custom form.

:::caution
After you remove a custom field or change its **Fieldname**, RareBooks does not show its old values again.
:::
