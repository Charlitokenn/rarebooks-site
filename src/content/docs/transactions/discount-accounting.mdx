A discount decreases the price of an item on an invoice. RareBooks applies discounts to item rows and records them in a discount account. Your income accounts then show the full price, and the discount account shows the discounts.

You can give discounts on sales invoices, purchase invoices, and sales quotes.

## Enable Discount Accounting

1. Click **Setup** > **Settings** in the sidebar.
2. In the **General** tab, turn on **Enable Discount Accounting**.
3. Optional: In **Accounts**, select the **Discount Account**.
4. Click **Save** and click **Yes** to reload RareBooks.

The **Discount Account** must be a ledger account with the root type **Income**. If you do not select an account, RareBooks uses the account **Discounts**. If this account does not exist, RareBooks makes it.

:::caution
You cannot turn off discount accounting after you enable it.
:::

## Give a Discount on an Item

1. Open a draft invoice or quote.
2. Click the **Edit row** button at the end of the item row.
3. Enter the **Discount Percent**.
4. Check the **Discounted Amount**.

![Row panel of a draft sales invoice with Discount Percent 10, and the Discounted Amount and Taxed Amount that RareBooks calculates.](/images/docs/books-docs-discount-accounting-1-row-discount-v4.png)

To enter an amount instead of a percent, select **Set Discount Amount**, and then enter the **Discount Amount**. This amount is for the full row, not for one unit. It cannot be more than the **Amount** of the row.

The **Discount** field in **Tax and Totals** shows the total of all the item discounts. RareBooks does not have a discount for the whole invoice. To give the same discount on all items, enter it in each row.

A [pricing rule](/masters/pricing-rule/) or a [coupon code](/masters/coupon-code/) can also fill the item discounts. Pricing rules need discount accounting. In the [Point of Sale](/transactions/point-of-sale/), the cashier can change discounts only when **Can Change Discount** is on.

## Discount Before or After Tax

By default, RareBooks applies the discount before the tax. It calculates the tax on the amount after the discount.

To apply the discount after the tax, open the **Settings** tab of the invoice and select **Apply Discount After Tax**. RareBooks then calculates the tax on the full amount, and applies the discount to the amount with the tax.

For example, an item has an amount of 1,000.00, a tax of 10%, and a discount amount of 100.00:

| | Discount Before Tax | Discount After Tax |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Net Total | 1,000.00 | 1,000.00 |
| Tax (10%) | 90.00 | 100.00 |
| Discount | 100.00 | 100.00 |
| Grand Total | 990.00 | 1,000.00 |

## Ledger Entries

When you submit an invoice with discounts, RareBooks posts the item amounts before the discount. It posts the total discount to the discount account. For the example above with the discount before the tax, a sales invoice makes these entries:

| Account | Debit | Credit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The **Account** of the invoice (receivable) | 990.00 | |
| Discount account | 100.00 | |
| Income account of the item | | 1,000.00 |
| Tax account | | 90.00 |

A purchase invoice makes the opposite entries. It credits the discount account with the discount. A return reverses the discount entries of the original invoice.
