You can make a sales invoice or a purchase invoice in the currency of the customer or the supplier. RareBooks shows and prints the invoice in that currency. It keeps your accounts in the company currency.

You select the company currency in the setup wizard. It shows in the **System** tab of **Settings**, but you cannot change it there. All ledger entries, payments, and reports use the company currency.

## Enable a Currency

The **Currency** field of a party shows only the currencies that are enabled. If a currency is not in the list, a **System Manager** can enable it in Frappe Desk:

1. Open `https://<your-site>/app/currency`.
2. Open the currency.
3. Select **Enabled**.
4. Click **Save**.
5. Reload RareBooks.

## Set the Party Currency

1. Open the customer or the supplier.
2. Select the **Currency**.
3. Click **Save**.

If you leave **Currency** empty, RareBooks sets it to the company currency. For more information, see [Party](/masters/party/).

## Creating a Multi Currency Invoice

1. Make a new sales invoice or purchase invoice.
2. Select a party that has a different currency. RareBooks sets the invoice currency to the party currency and gets the exchange rate.
3. Check the exchange rate.
4. Add the items.
5. Save and submit the invoice.

On a computer, the exchange rate shows in the page header, such as `1 USD = 83.12 INR`. On a phone, the **Exchange Rate** field is at the end of the first section of the form. For the other steps, see [Sales Invoices](/transactions/sales-invoice/) and [Purchase Invoices](/transactions/purchase-invoice/#creating-purchase-invoices).

## Exchange Rate

RareBooks gets the rate for the **Date** of the invoice from a public exchange rate service. If the service has no rate, or RareBooks cannot connect to it, a warning tells you to enter the rate.

![Sales invoice in US dollars with the rate 1 USD = 87.6 INR in the page header, and the Base Grand Total in rupees.](/images/docs/books-docs-multi-currency-invoicing-1-usd-invoice-v4.png)

To change the rate, type the amount in the company currency in the right box, and press `Enter`. To enter the rate in the other direction, click the **Swap currencies** button. The rate must be more than zero. After you submit the invoice, you cannot change the rate.

:::caution
When you change the **Date**, RareBooks gets the rate for the new date. This rate replaces a rate that you typed. Check the rate again.
:::

## Amounts on the Invoice

Item rates, totals, and taxes are in the party currency. The printed invoice and the amount in words are also in the party currency. **Base Grand Total** and **Outstanding Amount** are in the company currency. The invoice lists show these two amounts.

The rate of an item is in the company currency. When you add the item, RareBooks divides the item rate by the exchange rate.

## Ledger Entries and Payments

RareBooks multiplies each amount by the exchange rate and makes the ledger entries in the company currency. It posts rounding differences to the **Round Off Account** in the **General** settings. Make sure that this account is set before you submit a multi currency invoice.

Payments are in the company currency. When you make a payment from the invoice, RareBooks fills the outstanding amount in the company currency. See [Payments](/transactions/payments/).

RareBooks does not calculate exchange gains or losses. To record one, make a [journal entry](/transactions/journal-entries/).
