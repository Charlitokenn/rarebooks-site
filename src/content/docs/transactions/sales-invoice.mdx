A sales invoice is the bill that you send to a customer for a sale. When you submit it, RareBooks records the income and the amount that the customer owes you.

## Create a Sales Invoice

Before you start, add your customers and your sales items. For the steps, see [Create Initial Entries](/getting-started/create-initial-entries/).

1. Click **Sales** > **Sales Invoices** in the sidebar.
2. Click **New**.
3. Select the **Customer**. RareBooks fills the **Account** from the customer.
4. In **Items**, click **Add row**.
5. Select the **Item**. RareBooks fills the rate and the tax of the item.
6. Enter the **Qty**.
7. Add a row for each item that you sell.
8. Click **Save**.
9. Click **Submit** and click **Yes**.

After you submit the invoice, you cannot edit it. For more information, see [Save and Submit Documents](/getting-started/getting-started-with-rarebooks/#save-and-submit-documents). You can also make an invoice from a submitted [sales quote](/transactions/sales-quotes/). Invoices from the [Point of Sale](/transactions/point-of-sale/) also show in the list.

When you submit the invoice, RareBooks debits the **Account** of the invoice with the grand total. It credits the income account of each item and each tax account. A discount goes to the discount account. See [Discount Accounting](/transactions/discount-accounting/).

## Invoice Fields

| Field | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Account | The receivable account of the customer, such as Debtors. |
| Price List | Shows when price lists are on. RareBooks takes the item rates from this list. See [Price List](/masters/price-list/). |
| Taxes | The tax amount for each tax account. RareBooks calculates it from the **Tax** of each item row. |
| Base Grand Total | The grand total in your company currency. Shows only when the customer uses a different currency. See [Multi-Currency Invoicing](/transactions/multicurrency-accounting/). |
| Outstanding Amount | The amount that the customer still owes. |
| Notes | Text that shows on the printed invoice. New invoices get the **Sales Invoice Terms** from the settings. |

![Sales invoice list with the statuses Paid, Unpaid, Partly Paid, and Saved, and the outstanding amounts.](/images/docs/books-docs-sales-invoices-2-list-v4.png)

In an item row, RareBooks fills the rate from the item or from the price list. If you enter a rate, RareBooks keeps your rate. To charge no tax on a row, clear its **Tax**. To see the other fields of a row, such as the income **Account**, click the **Edit row** button at the end of the row.

The **Loyalty Points Redemption**, **Coupons**, and **Pricing Rule Detail** sections show only when their feature is on. See [Loyalty Program](/transactions/loyalty-program/), [Coupon Code](/masters/coupon-code/), and [Pricing Rule](/masters/pricing-rule/).

The **Settings** tab shows only when one of its fields applies:

| Field | Shows When |
| --- | --- |
| Apply Discount After Tax | Discount accounting is on. See [Discount Accounting](/transactions/discount-accounting/). |
| Make Payment On Submit | A **Sales Payment Account** is set. See [Automatic Payments](#automatic-payments). |
| Make Shipment On Submit | Inventory is on and a **Shipment Location** is set. See [Automatic Shipments](#automatic-shipments). |

After you submit an invoice, its status is **Unpaid**, **Partly Paid**, or **Paid**. A return has the status **Return**. Its original invoice gets the status **Return Issued** when it is fully returned, or when it has no outstanding amount. Otherwise, the original keeps its payment status, such as **Unpaid**.

## Receive a Payment

1. Open the submitted sales invoice.
2. Click **Create** > **Payment**. RareBooks fills the **Amount** with the outstanding amount.
3. Check the **Payment Method** and the **To Account**.
4. Click **Save**.
5. Click **Submit** and click **Yes**.

![Unpaid sales invoice with the Create menu open: Payment, Return, Duplicate, and New entry.](/images/docs/books-docs-sales-invoices-1-create-menu-v4.png)

RareBooks decreases the **Outstanding Amount** of the invoice and changes its status. To pay less than the outstanding amount, turn on **Enable Partial Payment** in the **General** settings. For more information, see [Payments](/transactions/payments/).

### Automatic Payments

To make the payment when you submit the invoice, select a **Sales Payment Account** in the **Auto Payments** section of the **Defaults** settings. The list shows only cash and bank accounts.

Each new sales invoice then has **Make Payment On Submit** selected in its **Settings** tab. When you submit the invoice, RareBooks submits a payment of the outstanding amount to this account. To skip the payment for one invoice, clear **Make Payment On Submit** before you submit.

## Print a Sales Invoice

For the steps, see [Print a Document](/settings/print-templates/#print-a-document). RareBooks has two templates for sales invoices: **Business - Sales Invoice** and **Business-POS - Sales Invoice**. To set the template that opens first, select the **Sales Invoice Print Template** in the **Defaults** settings.

## Shipments

When inventory is on, a shipment takes the sold items out of your stock. RareBooks ships only items that have **Track Inventory** selected. For more information, see [Shipment](/inventory/shipment/).

### Automatic Shipments

To ship the items when you submit the invoice, select a **Shipment Location** in the **Auto Stock Transfer** section of the **Defaults** settings.

Each new invoice then has **Make Shipment On Submit** selected in its **Settings** tab. When you submit the invoice, RareBooks submits a shipment from the **Shipment Location**. If an item does not have enough stock, the **Insufficient quantity** dialog opens. Click **Yes** to submit the invoice without the shipment, or click **No** to stop.

To ship the items later, open the invoice and click **Create** > **Shipment**. **Stock Not Shipped** shows the quantity that is not shipped yet.

## Returns

A return is a sales invoice with negative quantities for items that a customer gives back. It reverses the ledger entries of the original invoice.

To make returns, turn on **Enable Invoice Returns** in the **General** settings. You cannot turn it off later.

1. Open the submitted sales invoice.
2. Click **Create** > **Return**. A new invoice opens with the items that earlier returns did not take back.
3. Change the quantities to the quantities that the customer returned. You can enter positive numbers.
4. Remove the rows of the items that the customer did not return.
5. Click **Save**.
6. Click **Submit** and click **Yes**.

To make a return manually, make a new invoice and select the original invoice in **Return Against** in **References**. The customer must be the same as on the original invoice, and each item must be on it. The returned quantities cannot be more than the invoiced quantities. A return cannot credit an item at more than it was sold for.

The outstanding amount of a return is negative. To pay the money back to the customer, open the return and click **Create** > **Payment**.

## Cancel a Sales Invoice

Only a **Books Manager** or a **System Manager** can delete a sales invoice. See [Roles and Permissions](/getting-started/roles-and-permissions/).

When you cancel an invoice, RareBooks reverses its ledger entries. It also cancels the payments and the automatic shipment of the invoice. A submitted return, or a shipment that you made with **Create** > **Shipment**, stops the cancellation. Cancel these entries first.

To make a corrected invoice, open the cancelled invoice and click **Create** > **Duplicate**.

## More Actions

| Action | Where |
| --- | --- |
| Linked entries | **…** > **Linked entries** in the page header, or `Ctrl + L`. Shows the payments, shipments, returns, and other entries of the invoice. |
| Accounting entries | **View** > **Accounting entries**. Opens the [General Ledger](/financial-reports/general-ledger/) for the invoice. |
| Print | The printer icon in the page header, or `Ctrl + P`. |
| Save or Submit | The main button in the page header, or `Ctrl + S`. |

![Phone view of an unpaid sales invoice with Print and Receive payment at the bottom of the form.](/images/docs/books-docs-sales-invoices-3-phone-form-v4.png)

On macOS, use `Cmd` instead of `Ctrl`. On a phone, **Linked entries** and **Accounting entries** are in the **…** menu in the header.
