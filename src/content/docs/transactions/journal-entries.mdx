A journal entry records a transaction directly in your accounts. Use it for transactions that are not a sale or a purchase, such as a transfer from a bank account to the cash account.

Only a **Books Manager** or a **System Manager** can open journal entries. For more information, see [Roles and Permissions](/getting-started/roles-and-permissions/).

## Create a Journal Entry

1. Click **Common** > **Journal Entry** in the sidebar.
2. Click **New**.
3. Select the **Entry Type**.
4. Make sure that the **Date** is correct.
5. In **Account Entries**, add one row for each account.
6. In each row, select the **Account** and enter the **Debit** or the **Credit** amount.
7. Click **Save**.
8. Click **Submit** and click **Yes**.

The **Account** list shows only ledger accounts. To add an account, see [Create a New Account](/getting-started/chart-of-accounts/#create-a-new-account). The optional **Reference Number** and **Reference Date** in the **References** section are for an outside document, such as a check.

:::tip[Balancing Amount]
When you add a row, RareBooks fills it with the amount that balances the entry. For example, enter a debit of `5,000` in the first row. Then add a row. The new row gets a credit of `5,000`.
:::

### Rules for a Journal Entry

- Each row has a debit or a credit, but not both.
- The amounts are not negative.
- The total of the debits is equal to the total of the credits.

If the entry does not obey a rule, RareBooks shows an error and does not save the entry.

## Entry Types

The entry type tells what kind of transaction the entry records. It does not change the ledger entries. Some common types:

| Entry Type | Use |
| --- | --- |
| **Opening Entry** | The opening balances of your accounts when you move from a different system. See [Setup Opening Balances](/getting-started/setup-opening-balances/). |
| **Contra Entry** | A transfer between your cash account and your bank accounts. |
| **Bank Entry** | A transaction that you pay or receive through a bank account. |
| **Cash Entry** | A transaction that you pay or receive in cash. A POS shift also makes this type. See [Close the Shift](/transactions/point-of-sale/#close-the-shift). |
| **Write Off Entry** | An amount that you will not collect. |
| **Depreciation Entry** | The decrease in the value of a fixed asset. |

The other types are **Journal Entry**, **Credit Card Entry**, **Debit Note**, **Credit Note**, and **Excise Entry**.

## Cancel or Delete a Journal Entry

You cannot edit a submitted entry. When you cancel an entry, RareBooks reverses its ledger entries. To make a corrected entry, open the cancelled entry and click **Create** > **Duplicate**. A copy opens as a new draft.

You can delete a draft entry or a cancelled entry. When you delete a cancelled entry, RareBooks also deletes its ledger entries and their reversals. You cannot undo a delete.
