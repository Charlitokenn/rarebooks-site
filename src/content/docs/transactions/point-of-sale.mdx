Use the POS to sell over the counter. RareBooks records each sale as a sales invoice and records each payment. Each cashier sells in their own POS shift, which starts and ends with a count of the cash in their drawer.

## Turn On POS

1. Click **Setup** > **Settings** in the sidebar.
2. In the **General** tab, turn on **Enable Inventory**.
3. Click **Save** and click **Yes** to reload RareBooks.
4. Open **Settings** again. In the **Inventory** tab, turn on **Enable Point of Sale**.
5. Click **Save** and click **Yes** to reload RareBooks.

**POS** now shows in the sidebar, and **Settings** has a **POS Settings** tab. Only a Books Manager or a System Manager can change the POS settings. You cannot turn off POS while a cashier has a shift open.

:::caution[POS Without Inventory]
**Enable POS Without Inventory** in the **General** tab turns on POS without **Enable Inventory**. It also turns on barcodes, batches, serial numbers, and UOM conversion. You cannot turn off these settings later.
:::

## Set Up POS

### POS Settings

The setup wizard fills the inventory and the accounts in the **POS Settings** tab. Check them before your first sale.

![POS Settings tab with inventory, counter cash account, write off account, POS UI, default account, item visibility, and the switches.](/images/docs/books-docs-transactional-entries-1-pos-settings-v4.png)

| Field | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Inventory | The location that POS sales take stock from. |
| Counter Cash Account | The cash account of the drawer. Cash payments for POS sales go to this account while the cashier has an open shift. Without an open shift, they go to the account of the payment method. |
| Write Off Account | The expense account for the cash difference when you close a shift. |
| Default Account | The receivable account of POS invoices. |
| POS UI | **Classic** edits the values in the cart row. **Modern** opens a keypad for each value. |
| Item Visibility | **Inventory Items** (the default), **Non-Inventory Items**, or **All Items**. |
| Ignore Pricing Rule | POS sales do not use pricing rules or coupons. |

For scale barcodes that hold the item code and the weight in grams, turn on **Weight Enabled Barcode**. Then fill the **Barcode** section. This section shows only when **Enable Barcodes** is on.

### POS Profile

A POS profile keeps the settings of one counter or location, such as its **Inventory**, **POS Customer**, and receipt template. Each cashier selects a profile when they open a shift. Its values replace the same values in POS Settings and in the **Defaults** tab for the sales of that shift. The **POS Profile** field in POS Settings sets the profile that a new shift starts with.

For example, a shop with two locations makes one profile for each location, each with its own **Inventory**. The cashiers at each location select the profile of their location. A POS sale always prints with the receipt template of the profile that it was sold with.

### POS Defaults

The **Defaults** tab of **Settings** has the **POS Customer**, the button colors, and the **POS Print Template** for receipts. It also has **Cash Denominations**, the notes and coins that the shift dialogs count. A POS profile replaces these values, except **Cash Denominations**.

## Open a Shift

1. Click **POS** in the sidebar. If you do not have an open shift, the **Open POS shift** dialog opens.
2. If the site has POS profiles, select the **POS Profile** of your counter.
3. In **Cash in drawer**, enter the **Count** of each note and coin.
4. In **Opening amounts**, enter the amount of each other payment method, if any.
5. Click **Open shift**.

Each user has their own shift, so several cashiers can sell at the same time. A user can have only one open shift. Give each cashier their own drawer, because each shift counts only the payments of its cashier. RareBooks submits a journal entry that moves the opening cash from the **Cash** account to the **Counter Cash Account**. When these accounts are the same, there is no journal entry.

## Make a Sale

1. To add an item, click it, or scan or type its code and press `Enter`.
2. To sell to a different customer, select the **Customer** at the top of the cart.
3. To change a quantity, click the plus or minus button in the row. To edit other values, click the item name.
4. Click **Pay**.
5. In **Payment method**, click a method, such as **Cash**.
6. Check **Amount paid**. For a method that is not cash, enter **Ref. / Cheque No.**
7. Click **Pay**, or click **Pay and print** to also print the receipt.

![POS with the item list and a cart for Riya Kapoor: three items, a free notebook from a pricing rule, and the Coupon, Loyalty, and Price list buttons.](/images/docs/books-docs-transactional-entries-2-cart-v4.png)

![Payment dialog with Cash selected, $25.00 tendered, and Change to return $4.24.](/images/docs/books-docs-transactional-entries-3-payment-dialog-v4.png)

RareBooks submits the invoice and a payment, and clears the cart. Inventory items ship from the POS inventory.

- You cannot add an inventory item that has no stock.
- You can submit a POS invoice, or take a payment for a POS invoice, only while you have an open shift.
- If the customer gives more cash, the dialog shows **Change to return**. The payment records only the amount due.
- To take less than the full amount, turn on **Enable Partial Payment** in the **General** tab of **Settings**. **Submit unpaid** submits the invoice without a payment.

To keep a sale for later, click **Hold**. To open it again, click **Held** in the page header. All POS invoices also show in [Sales Invoices](/transactions/sales-invoice/).

## Return a Sale

Returns need **Enable Invoice Returns** in the **General** tab of **Settings**.

1. Click **Return** in the page header.
2. Click the invoice, and then click **Create return**. The cart shows its items with negative quantities.
3. If the customer returns only some items, change the quantities or remove rows.
4. Click **Refund**.
5. Select the payment method, and click **Refund**.

To submit the return without a refund, click **Submit without refund**.

A return does not need an open shift. Without an open shift, the refund comes from the account of the payment method, not from the **Counter Cash Account**.

## Coupons, Loyalty Points, and Price Lists

The **Coupon**, **Loyalty**, and **Price list** buttons above the totals show only when their feature is on. Coupons and loyalty points also need a customer and at least one item. If a change to the cart makes a coupon not apply, RareBooks removes the coupon and shows a warning. For more information, see [Coupon Code](/masters/coupon-code/), [Loyalty Program](/transactions/loyalty-program/), and [Price List](/masters/price-list/).

## Item Enquiry

Use an item enquiry to record an item that a customer asks for. The **Item enquiry** button shows in the page header when **Enable Item Enquiry** is on.

1. Click **Item enquiry**.
2. Select the **Item**, and enter a **Description**.
3. Click **Submit**.

To see all enquiries, search for `Item Enquiry` in [Quick Search](/getting-started/quick-search/).

## Close the Shift

1. Click **Close shift** in the page header.
2. In **Count the drawer**, enter the **Count** of each note and coin.
3. In **Closing amounts**, enter the **Closing** amount of each payment method that is not cash.
4. Check the **Difference** of each method.
5. Click **Close shift**.

![Close POS shift dialog with the drawer count and the closing amounts of each method, with a cash difference of -2.00.](/images/docs/books-docs-transactional-entries-4-close-shift-v4.png)

**Expected** is the opening amount, plus the POS payments that you took during the shift, minus the refunds that you gave. The device must be online to close a shift. RareBooks submits a journal entry that moves the counted cash from the **Counter Cash Account** to the **Cash** account. A cash difference goes to the **Write Off Account**.

### Close a Cashier's Shift

A Books User or a Books Manager can close the open shift of a different cashier, for example when the cashier forgets to close it.

1. Search for `POS Closing Shift` in [Quick Search](/getting-started/quick-search/) and open the **List** result.
2. Click **New**.
3. In **Opening Shift**, select the shift. The list shows the open shifts, grouped by cashier.
4. Enter the counted cash and the closing amounts.
5. Click **Save**.
6. Click **Submit** and click **Yes**.

To see all shifts, search for `POS Opening Shift`. The list shows the **Cashier**, the **POS Profile**, and the **Status** of each shift, such as **Open** or **Closed**. A Books Cashier sees only their own shifts.

## Use POS on a Phone

- The cart bar at the bottom shows the number of items and the grand total. Tap it to open the cart.
- In the cart, tap a row to change its **Rate** and **Discount %**. To change the batch, the serial numbers, or the unit, use a computer.
- The **…** button in the header has the other actions, such as **Return Sales Invoice** and **Close POS shift**.
- Tap the back arrow in the header to leave the POS.

![Phone POS cart sheet with two items, the totals, Hold, and Pay.](/images/docs/books-docs-transactional-entries-5-phone-cart-v4.png)

## Keyboard Shortcuts

Use `Cmd` instead of `Ctrl` on macOS.

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Hold `Q` and type digits | Set the quantity of the selected row, or of the last row |
| `Ctrl + Shift + H` | Open the **Invoices** dialog |
| `Ctrl + Shift + S` | Hold the sale |
| `Ctrl + Shift + P` | Open the payment dialog |
| `Ctrl + Shift + Backspace` | Close the open dialog. If no dialog is open, clear the cart. |
