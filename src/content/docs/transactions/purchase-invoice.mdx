A purchase invoice records a bill that a supplier sends you for goods or services. When you submit it, RareBooks makes the ledger entries and records the amount that you owe the supplier.

Before you make purchase invoices, add your suppliers, your purchase items, and your taxes. For the steps, see [Create Initial Entries](/getting-started/create-initial-entries/).

## Creating Purchase Invoices

1. Click **Purchases** > **Purchase Invoices** in the sidebar.
2. Click **New**.
3. Select the **Supplier**. RareBooks fills the **Account** with the payable account of the supplier.
4. In **Items**, click **Add row** and select the **Item**.
5. Enter the **Qty**.
6. If the supplier charges a different price, change the **Rate**.
7. Add a row for each of the other items.
8. Click **Save**.
9. Click **Submit** and click **Yes**.

After you submit the invoice, you cannot edit it. For more information, see [Save and Submit Documents](/getting-started/getting-started-with-rarebooks/#save-and-submit-documents).

You can also open a supplier, click the **…** button, and click **Create purchase**.

### Items and Taxes

The item list shows the items with the purpose **Purchases** or **Both**. When you select an item, RareBooks fills the **Rate**, the **Tax**, and the expense **Account** of the row from the item. If you type a different rate, RareBooks keeps your rate. To see all the fields of a row, click **Edit row** at the end of the row. For more information, see [Items](/masters/items/).

![Draft purchase invoice from Ink & Nib Supplies with two item rows and the IGST tax.](/images/docs/books-docs-purchase-invoices-1-draft-invoice-v4.png)

RareBooks calculates the taxes of each row from its **Tax**. You cannot edit the **Taxes** table. To change a tax, change the **Tax** of the item row. To charge no tax on a row, clear its **Tax**. For more information, see [Taxes](/masters/taxes/) and [Discount Accounting](/transactions/discount-accounting/).

### What Submit Does

When you submit a purchase invoice, RareBooks makes these ledger entries:

| Account | Entry |
| --- | --- |
| The payable **Account** of the supplier | Credit with the grand total |
| The expense account of each item | Debit with the item amount |
| Each tax account | Debit with the tax amount |

The **Outstanding Amount** is the amount that you still owe. The invoice status is **Unpaid**, **Partly Paid**, or **Paid**. To see the ledger entries, click **View** > **Accounting entries**.

## Making Purchase Payments

1. Open the submitted purchase invoice.
2. Click **Create** > **Payment**. RareBooks fills the **Amount** with the outstanding amount.
3. Check the **Payment Method** and the **From Account**.
4. If the payment method is not **Cash**, enter the **Ref. / Cheque No.**
5. Click **Save**.
6. Click **Submit** and click **Yes**.

RareBooks updates the **Outstanding Amount** and the status of the invoice. To pay less than the outstanding amount, turn on **Enable Partial Payment** in the **General** settings. For more information, see [Payments](/transactions/payments/).

### Auto Payments

To make the payment when you submit the invoice, select the **Purchase Payment Account** in the **Defaults** settings. The list shows only cash and bank accounts.

Each new purchase invoice then has **Make Payment On Submit** selected in its **Settings** tab. When you submit the invoice, RareBooks submits a payment for the full amount from this account. The payment uses the invoice number as its reference. To skip the payment for one invoice, clear **Make Payment On Submit** before you submit.

## Purchase Receipts

When inventory is on, a purchase invoice can also receive the items into your stock. To do this, select the **Purchase Receipt Location** in the **Defaults** settings.

Each new purchase invoice then has **Make Purchase Receipt On Submit** selected in its **Settings** tab. When you submit the invoice, RareBooks submits a purchase receipt for the items that have **Track Inventory** selected. The invoice shows the receipt in **Back Reference**.

To receive the items later, open the submitted invoice and click **Create** > **Purchase Receipt**. **Stock Not Received** shows the quantity that is not received yet. For more information, see [Purchase Receipt](/inventory/purchase-receipt/).

## Purchase Invoice Return

A return records the items that you send back to the supplier. It reverses the ledger entries of the original invoice for the returned items. To make returns, turn on **Enable Invoice Returns** in the **General** settings.

1. Open the submitted purchase invoice.
2. Click **Create** > **Return**. A new invoice opens with the items that earlier returns did not take back.
3. Change the quantities to the quantities that you return. You can enter positive numbers.
4. Remove the rows of the items that you do not return.
5. Click **Save**.
6. Click **Submit** and click **Yes**.

To make a return manually, make a new invoice and select the original invoice in **Return Against**. The supplier must be the same, and each item must be on the original invoice. The returned quantities and value cannot be more than the original invoice.

The return has the status **Return**. The original invoice gets **Return Issued** when it is fully returned, or when it has no outstanding amount. Otherwise, it keeps its payment status, such as **Unpaid**. The outstanding amount of a return is negative. To record the refund from the supplier, open the return and click **Create** > **Payment**.

## Cancelling a Purchase Invoice

When you cancel an invoice, RareBooks reverses its ledger entries. It also cancels the payments that settle the invoice and the purchase receipt that the invoice made on submit. If a submitted return refers to the invoice, cancel the return first.

:::caution
If a payment also settles other invoices, cancelling it changes the outstanding amount of those invoices too.
:::

To make a corrected copy, open the cancelled invoice and click **Create** > **Duplicate**.
